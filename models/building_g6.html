Building

Setbacks
Front 8′-0″ (2.44 m), sides 4′-6″ (1.37 m), rear 5′-0″ (1.52 m). Required: front 1.5 m, sides 1.00 m, rear 1.25 m (DBR rule 41(1), Table 1).
Levels
Ground floor +1′-6″ above the road (provisional). GF 12′ (parking, entrance), 1F–6F 10′ (dwellings). Roof slab 72′ (21.95 m) above the ground floor.
Structure
RC frame. Columns 12″ × 18″, shown at the ground floor only; slab 125 mm; core walls 200 mm. All provisional.
Stair
One stair, flights 1.15 m wide. GF 22 risers × 166 mm, 1F–6F 18 × 169 mm, treads 250 mm (DBR rule 57: risers ≤ 175, treads ≥ 225).
Lift
One lift, required because the top floor roof is above 18 m (DBR rule 59(1)). Shaft about 2.06 × 1.87 m clear, pit 1.5 m, machine room on the roof (provisional; no lift model chosen).
Roof
Parapet 3′-6″. Stair head room and lift machine room 10′. Overhead water tank on top, 15′ (4.57 m) above the roof (limit 5 m, DBR rule 49(gha)).
