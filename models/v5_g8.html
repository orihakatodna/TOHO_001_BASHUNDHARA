Plot
45′ × 64′ (13.72 × 19.51 m). Road 25′ (7.62 m) on the south. Setbacks: front 2.50 m, rear 2.01 m, sides 1.36 m.
Building
10.995 × 15.000 m, G+8. Floors 1F–8F: dwelling 9.75 m deep, core 5.25 m behind it on the right; the rear left (5.39 × 5.25 m) is open above the ground floor (“Roof”).
Levels
GF 12′ (3.658 m), 1F–8F 10′ (3.048 m). Ground floor +1′-6″ above the road (assumed). Roof 28.50 m above the road, parapet 1.14 m, stair room 2.90 m high: top 31.40 m (H ≤ 33 m).
Finishes
West face fair-faced concrete (formwork 1,800 × 900, form ties @600 × @450) with one long window on each floor; south face exposed brick (English bond). East and north faces concrete (assumed: no drawing). Partitions brick, 125 mm, exposed.
Dwelling
B.R.2 and B.R.3, each with a bath; Master Bedroom with bath and closet; Living Dining entered at ENT. from the lift hall; Kitchen; accessible bath; Terrace with planters. Brick planter balconies north of B.R.3 and south of the Master Bedroom. Doors as drawn in v5: swing doors 0.6–0.7 m, a sliding door to the accessible bath, and sliding doors from the Master Bedroom to the walk-in closet.
Furniture
As drawn in the v5 plan of floors 1F–8F, at the sizes of the CM-01 furniture list (as CM-04/05): beds, sofa, chairs, tables, TV units, kitchen counters, fridge, WCs, basins. The drawn pieces are smaller than those sizes, so some are moved or left out. The two baths drawn in v5 are shown as shower areas (the list has no bath). Shown on the floor chosen in “Floors shown”; fixed in place.
Ground floor
Parking for 6 (1–4 on the east, 5–6 under the roof at the rear left), brick driveway, steps and tactile paving from the road to the lift hall, lift, stair, stock under the stair.
Stair, lift
One stair, flights 1.15 m: GF 22 risers × 166 mm, 1F–8F 18 × 169 mm, treads 250 mm; up the east flight, back on the west (as v5). Lift 1,600 kg (RevG).
Structure
RC. Four corner columns (v5 plan: “Wide Span”, “No Column”) and one more at the ground floor. Slab 125 mm, beams 250 × 600 mm (assumed).
Areas
Typical floor 136.59 m² (table 136.60), building area 164.91 m² (table 164.92), plot 267.56 m² (table 267.56).

Concept model for discussion, not for construction. Where the v5 drawings disagree, this model takes: the ground-floor height as written (12′; drawn about 3.17 m); the west windows from the elevation (one long window; the plan shows one per room); the north planter from the plan; the south planter 3.3 m wide (the RevG line is 5.6 m). Window sizes, the east and north faces, slab, beams, levels and the positions of doors and furniture (read from the v5 plan at scale) are assumptions. Not checked: building and fire rules, structure.
